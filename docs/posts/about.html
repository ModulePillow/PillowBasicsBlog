About PillowBasics

PillowBasics is a free, open-source game engine maintained by the ModulePillow community.

Our Goals

  • Keep the engine small and understandable — no magic, no black boxes
  • Provide excellent documentation and beginner-friendly tutorials
  • Build a welcoming community for game developers of all skill levels
